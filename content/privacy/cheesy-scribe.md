Cheesy Scribe is a cheese-tasting notebook for Android made by TK ForgeWorks LLC ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to the information you put into the app.

The short version: **your notes stay on your phone.** The only thing that ever leaves it is feedback you choose to send us from the app. We don't sell or share any of your data.

## What the app stores

Everything you enter is saved only on your device, in the app's private storage:

- your tasting notes (cheese names, creameries and origins, ratings, flavor and texture scores, prices, tasting dates and the notes you write)
- your settings (theme, units and the display name you choose)
- your recent searches

There's no account and no sign-in, and none of this is ever sent to us. Your notes, settings and searches are not part of the feedback described below.

## What we collect

Only the feedback you send us (see below). Cheesy Scribe doesn't contain analytics, advertising or tracking code, and it never sends anything in the background.

## Feedback you send

The About screen has a **Send feedback** form. Nothing is sent unless you fill it in and tap **Send**. When you do, the app sends:

- what the feedback is about (a bug, an idea, something confusing, or just saying hi)
- the rating you pick, if you pick one
- the comments you write
- your email address, only if you enter one so we can reply
- the app's version, your Android version and your phone's model (for example "Google Pixel 9"), shown on the form before you send

It does not send your notes, your display name or any identifier for you or your device.

The form goes over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to our server at tkforgeworks.com, which runs on [Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/). The server emails it to our support inbox using [Resend](https://resend.com/legal/privacy-policy), an email delivery service. Both process it only to deliver it to us. As part of handling any web request, Cloudflare also sees your IP address, which we don't store.

We use feedback only to improve the app and, if you gave an email address, to reply to you. We keep it in our support inbox for as long as it's useful and never sell or share it.

## Things outside the app

- **Google Play.** When you install the app from Google Play, Google handles the download under [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Google gives developers anonymous, aggregated statistics such as install counts and crash rates. We can't use those to identify you.
- **Device backup.** If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include the app's data in your device backup. That backup is stored and managed by Google in your account; we can't access it.
- **Links.** The About screen links to [tkforgeworks.com](https://tkforgeworks.com) and this policy. Tapping a link opens it in your browser, outside the app.

## Deleting your data

Delete individual notes in the app, or remove everything by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the app. We never receive your notes, so there's nothing of them to delete on our side.

To have feedback you sent deleted, email [support@tkforgeworks.com](mailto:support@tkforgeworks.com) and tell us roughly when you sent it (and the email address, if you gave one). We'll delete it from our inbox.

## Children

Cheesy Scribe isn't directed at children under 13. It doesn't ask anyone for personal information; the feedback form's email field is optional, and if we learn that feedback came from a child under 13 we'll delete it.

## Changes to this policy

If the app starts handling data differently, for example by adding optional cloud backup, we'll update this page and the "Last updated" date before that version is released.
