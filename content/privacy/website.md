This policy covers [tkforgeworks.com](https://tkforgeworks.com), the website of TK ForgeWorks LLC ("we", "us"). Each app has its own privacy policy, linked from its project page and store listing.

The short version: **there are no accounts, forms or ads here.** We use Google Analytics to see which pages people read, and that's it.

## What we collect

- **Usage statistics.** The site uses Google Analytics, which sets cookies and records things like the pages you view, how you got here (the referring site), your browser and device type, and your approximate location (country or city). We use these reports to see what people read and what's worth writing more of. We don't use them to identify you, and we don't combine them with any other information. If you're visiting from the EU, the wider European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, Google Analytics stays off unless you choose Accept on the cookie banner.
- **Email you send us.** If you email us, we keep the message and your address so we can reply. We don't add you to a mailing list or share your email.

We don't sell, rent or share personal information, and the site shows no advertising.

## What stays in your browser

Your browser's local storage keeps two settings so the site remembers them: your light/dark theme and your answer on the cookie banner. They never leave your device.

## Third parties

- **Google Analytics.** Google processes the usage data above under [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). See [how Google uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).
- **Cloudflare.** The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Like any web host, Cloudflare handles your connection, including your IP address, to deliver pages and protect the site from abuse, under [Cloudflare's Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/). The site also asks Cloudflare which country you're connecting from, only to decide whether to show the cookie banner. We don't store that answer.
- **Links.** Links to other sites, such as GitHub, Instagram, Reddit, Discord and itch.io, take you off this site. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

## Your choices

- Use **Cookie settings** in the footer to accept or reject analytics at any time. Rejecting switches Google Analytics off and deletes its cookies.
- Block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site works fully without them.
- Install the [Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout) to stop Google Analytics on every site that uses it.
- Content blockers and your browser's tracking protection will also stop analytics here.

## Children

This site isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

## Changes to this policy

If the site starts collecting anything new, for example through a contact form, we'll update this page and the "Last updated" date first.
