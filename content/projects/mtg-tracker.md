A collection tracker for *Magic: The Gathering*, built with Java and Spring, because apparently I thought managing a card database was simpler than it actually is. (It is not. There are a lot of cards. They keep printing more.)

## Where it stands

The MVP works: collection management with a proper backend and an actual database. The grand vision — point a camera at a card and have it recognized automatically — involves computer vision, which involves several learning curves I haven't climbed yet. It's on the shelf, not in the bin: paused while the game-development projects have my attention, with a standing invitation to return.

## What I learned

Mostly that "simple CRUD app" is a phrase said only by people who haven't defined their data model yet.
