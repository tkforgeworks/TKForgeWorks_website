COG (Claude Observability GUI) is a desktop application for keeping tabs on Claude usage: live Cowork session tracking, usage trends and analytics, and chat import for digging through history. If you're going to work alongside an AI all day, you might as well have a dashboard telling you exactly how that's going — I spend my day job around instrumented systems, and apparently the habit followed me home.

## What it does

- Live tracking of Cowork sessions as they happen
- Trends and analytics over usage history
- Chat import, so past conversations become searchable data instead of digital sediment

## Where it stands

v1.2.0 has shipped, including UI polish and smarter feature visibility depending on the environment it's running in. Next on the list: preparing a proper Linux release, because the homelab deserves nice things too.
